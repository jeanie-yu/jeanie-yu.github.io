I love traveling, observing the world, and discovering the structures that quietly shape our everyday lives. Grids, patterns, and systems exist all around us, often unnoticed until we begin to pay attention. Once we recognize them, we start seeing them everywhere. This curiosity shapes how I see and approach graphic design.

I see graphic design as a structure built around a problem. Every challenge brings different information, perspectives, and possibilities. For me, design begins with understanding these relationships, questioning what already exists, and finding ways to bring clarity to complexity.

My practice moves between structure and experimentation. I am interested in how systems shape our perception and how challenging familiar patterns can reveal new perspectives. For me, design is about finding the right way to communicate a message, making it clear, meaningful, and memorable.
