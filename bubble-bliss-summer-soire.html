Bubble Bliss Summer Soirée

Poster Design | 2024

27.5"x 40"

A poster design for the "Bubble Bliss Summer Soirée" uses the vibrant color to promote colorful and joyful beachside events in California. With placement of event locations, the poster serves as a compelling invitation, attracting individuals to the sunset in California's beaches to join in an unforgettable summer afternoon soirée.
